OUR CUSTOMER CARE

When can I request an exchange?

If you receive the wrong product or an item arrives damaged, please contact GLOVAERA as soon as possible after receiving your order.

01 · Wrong product received

If the product you received is different from the product you ordered, contact us so we can review the order and help arrange the appropriate solution.

02 · Product arrived damaged

If your item appears damaged when you receive it, please take clear photos of the product and packaging and send them to us.

03 · Keep the product safely

Please keep the item, original packaging and any included accessories in their condition until our team reviews your request.

04 · Contact us first

Please do not send any product back before contacting GLOVAERA. We will explain the next step after reviewing your request.

BEFORE YOU CONTACT US

What should you send?

  • Your order ID
  • Your name and phone number
  • A short description of the issue
  • Clear photos of the product when relevant
PLEASE NOTE

Exchange requests are reviewed individually based on the condition of the item and the reason for the request.

For hygiene and product-safety reasons, items that have been significantly used, altered or damaged after delivery may not qualify for an exchange.
